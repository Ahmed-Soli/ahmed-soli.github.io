Some events stay with you long after the day is over. Google Summit Saudi Arabia was one of them.

With this year's summit canceled, I found myself looking back at last year's experience and remembering how much that day meant to me.

I had only recently joined Lean Technologies and relocated to Saudi Arabia. Walking into the summit, I was excited to explore the cloud and technology ecosystem, learn from industry experts, and meet people working on the challenges I was passionate about.

What I didn't expect was how many valuable connections I would make in a single day.

From conversations with DevOps and SRE engineers to meeting technical leads, engineering managers, and people from different corners of the industry, the summit offered much more than technical sessions. It was an opportunity to exchange ideas, share experiences, build professional relationships, and even make a few friends along the way.

The technical talks and activities were genuinely inspiring, while the exhibition area brought together some major names in the technology ecosystem, including Red Hat, MongoDB, IBM, and several Google Cloud partners and resellers.

But one of my favorite memories was finding myself on the other side of the conversation.

Instead of just attending and learning, I had the opportunity to represent Lean Technologies and explain open banking to many of the attendees. It was a great experience to introduce people to what we do, discuss the role of open banking in the fintech ecosystem, and share how technology is helping shape financial services in the region.

I left with a handful of business cards, new connections, new perspectives, and plenty of inspiration to take back to work.

Looking back, that day was a reminder that professional growth isn't only about the technologies we learn or the certifications we earn. It's also about the people we meet, the conversations we have, and the opportunities we create by showing up.

Hopefully, we'll be back at the next Google Summit Saudi Arabia with even more to learn, share, and build together. 🇸🇦
