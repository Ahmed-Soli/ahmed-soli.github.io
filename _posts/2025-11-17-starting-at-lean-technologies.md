I'm excited to share that I've started a new journey as Senior DevSecOps / SRE at Lean Technologies!

A huge thank you to Salma Adel Elzeny for guiding me through the recruitment process, to my colleagues Dmitrii Glazkov, Illia Shpak and Denis Korchuganov who ran the technical rounds with so much professionalism, and to our HR team Fer Loreno and Wafa Al-Amri who handled my relocation with care and precision.

I genuinely appreciate every effort that helped make this transition smooth.

For anyone unfamiliar, Lean Technologies is a pioneering open banking company operating across KSA, UAE, and with offices in Pune, London, and beyond.

The vision behind the company is incredible, led by a founder, Hisham Al-Falih, who truly sees the future of financial connectivity.

The culture here is something special: fast-growing, deeply collaborative, and maintaining an impressively low turnover in an industry that rarely sits still. With a strong Series B under its belt and momentum pushing toward unicorn status, it's an amazing time to join.

I'm grateful for the trust, and excited to be part of the journey ahead. Here's to building, scaling, and securing systems that support the next wave of open banking innovation.
